# Escenas de vínculo · A

## saul y dario · The Veyra riding school, at dawn
*a_saul_dario · nivel A*

- **narración**: Dario arrives in last night's clothes. Saúl is already grooming his horse.
- **saul**: You haven't slept.
- **dario**: Neither have you. But you hide it better.
- **saul**: I've been hiding things for thirty years. I'm good at it.
- **dario**: Hiding what?
- **narración**: Saúl puts down the brush. The horse snorts, impatient.
- **saul**: What happened to me in the study. What happens every time you walk into a room and I know where you are before I look.
- **dario** (ruborizada): Saúl.
- **saul**: I'm not asking for anything. My father, Tiria, duty: all of that's still there. I only wanted to say it aloud once, to someone other than the horse.
- **dario**: Do you know why I lost my composure over that loan? You watched my hands while I signed, and I forgot my own name.
- **saul** (alegre): You got the second letter wrong.
- **dario** (alegre): I know. I've never signed so badly or been so happy.
- **narración**: Neither moves any closer. Not yet. The horse is the only one who looks away.

> Saúl told Dario what he'd kept quiet for thirty years. He said it in a riding school at dawn, which is where Saúl says important things.

## irene y isolda · The Salvats' staircase, at night
*a_irene_isolda · nivel A*

- **narración**: Isolda has stayed until last again.
- **irene**: You'll miss your carriage.
- **isolda**: I've sent it away.
- **irene**: Why?
- **narración**: Isolda takes a deep breath. Her hands are trembling, and this time she doesn't hide them.
- **isolda**: Because all my life I've waited for others to speak for me. And this is something I can't ask anyone to say.
- **isolda** (ruborizada): Irene. I like you. Not as a friend. Like in the books that aren't lent out.
- **narración**: Irene doesn't answer at once. It's the first time she's been left without an exact phrase.
- **irene**: There are things about me you don't know. Things I do at night.
- **isolda**: I don't care what you do at night. I care that you looked for me for three weeks.
- **irene** (alegre): I'd have looked for three years.
- **isolda** (alegre): I said it first.
- **irene** (alegre): You said it first. I'm writing that down.

> Isolda told Irene how she felt before anyone could say it for her. It's the first time she has spoken first about something she wants.

## nilo y camila · The Ferrán conservatory
*a_nilo_camila · nivel A*

- **narración**: Camila is watering plants that belonged to her husband.
- **nilo**: I didn't know you liked plants.
- **camila**: I don't. Julián did. I water them so I don't have to decide whether to throw them out.
- **nilo**: I'll help.
- **camila** (molesta): See? There you go again. Offering before you know.
- **nilo**: This time I do know. I know I can't fix the ship, or the Miravés. And I still want to stay.
- **camila**: Why?
- **nilo** (ruborizada): Because you're the one person in Valcenda I don't want to rescue. I want to be beside you while you rescue yourself.
- **narración**: Camila sets down the watering can. She looks at him the way she looked at the punch that first night: working out what it will cost her.
- **camila** (alegre): That's the most romantic thing anyone's said to me since the axle. And the axle was you as well.
- **nilo**: There's something about me you still don't know.
- **camila**: I know. I see it in the way you look at the moon. When you want to tell me, I'll be here. Watering plants.

> Nilo promised Camila nothing. For the first time this season, that was what counted most.

## teo y celia · The bench at the edge of the dance floor, the same as the first time
*a_teo_celia · nivel A*

- **narración**: Teo is sitting. Celia is standing, counting nothing.
- **celia**: I've heard your father wants to marry you to money.
- **teo**: My father wants a lot of things. For the mills to work, for me to stop making jokes…
- **celia**: And what do you want?
- **teo**: That's the question I've been dodging all season.
- **celia**: Then stop dodging it. I've stopped counting my steps. Your turn.
- **narración**: Teo laughs. Then he goes quiet. Then he looks at her, and isn't laughing any more.
- **teo** (ruborizada): I want someone who laughs with me and not at me. And the only one who does is a lady who dances far too well for me.
- **celia**: I dance too well for everyone. That's not a reason.
- **teo**: Then what would be?
- **celia** (alegre): Saying it to me without a joke.
- **teo**: I like you, Celia. No joke.
- **celia** (ruborizada): Good. That was it.

> Teo told Celia something sincere, and didn't laugh afterwards. Celia says it's the hardest thing she's ever seen anyone do.

## dario y celia · The empty Academy, at dusk
*a_dario_celia · nivel A*

- **narración**: Dario is playing the piano badly, on purpose. Celia corrects him from the doorway.
- **celia**: That note's false.
- **dario**: Like me.
- **celia** (molesta): Don't say that.
- **dario**: It's true. All season I've played the charming heir. It's easier than being someone.
- **celia**: You don't do it with me.
- **dario**: With you I can't. You've seen me stumble with talent.
- **narración**: Celia sits beside him on the piano bench and plays the right note.
- **celia**: You told me you wanted to learn what it feels like not to pretend. Have you?
- **dario** (ruborizada): It feels like this. Fear. And wanting you not to leave.
- **celia** (alegre): I'm not leaving.
- **dario**: Celia, I like you. Not the applause. You.
- **celia**: I know. It's the only thing you've said all season without any charm.

> Dario told Celia something without a trace of charm. She says it's the best thing she's ever heard him say.

## teo y irene · The Salvats' library
*a_teo_irene · nivel A*

- **narración**: Teo has brought the notebook of verse. This time he opens it.
- **teo**: I promised I'd tell you if you believed me. No, the other way round: you'd tell me.
- **irene**: Read.
- **narración**: Teo reads. It's a short verse, with no easy rhyme and no joke.
- **irene**: Who is it for?
- **teo**: For a lady who never believes anything. I wrote it before I knew it was you. Then I knew.
- **irene**: It's accurate.
- **teo** (ruborizada): Is that good?
- **irene** (alegre): It's the best thing I say about anything.
- **irene**: Teo, there are things about me that could hurt you. And your friends.
- **teo**: My friends are a disaster and I'm an indebted clown. We're perfect for being hurt.
- **irene** (molesta): Don't laugh about this.
- **teo**: I'm not laughing. I like you, Irene. Night-time secrets and all.

> Teo read Irene a verse. She said it was accurate. Teo has been smiling for two days for no apparent reason.

## a y b · At the end of the evening, after everyone has gone
*a_generica · nivel A*

- **narración**: {a} and {b} are left alone. Neither says goodnight.
- **a**: I've been thinking about you for weeks, more than I should.
- **b** (ruborizada): And why say it now?
- **a**: Because the season is ending. And I don't want it to end without saying so.
- **b** (alegre): Then it won't end without my answer: me too.

> {a} and {b} no longer look for each other across the room: they find each other.
