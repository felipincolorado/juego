# Semana 1 · Opening

**Royal proclamation.** The Queen has opened the Season of the Seal. This autumn's matches will be sealed in the Archive.

## An evening of readings · día 27 · imprenta
*cena · plazas 2–3 · cualquiera*

> “Bring opinions, not curtsies.” Readings, wine and presses still warm.

Requisito: intelecto 3, chisme 3, presencia 2
- \+ The Salon Chronicle is born here: whoever shines will be in it
- − Mara asks a great deal, and writes it all down

**Carta**

*Dear Matchmaker,*

Every autumn I open the printing house one night before the noise begins. We read poems, argue over pamphlets and drink a wine finer than my trade ought to allow. Send me people who can hold a conversation. Those who can only nod, I already have on the presses.

*Mara Calce*

**Incidente (día 27)** · Mara hands out paper and ink: she wants every guest to write down who will be “the match of the season”. She means to print it.

- **Give her a harmless, entertaining rumour** (chisme, d 3)
  - Éxito: {mejor} hands her a rumour as delicious as it is false. Mara is delighted and stops asking.
  - A medias: The rumour half works: Mara notes it down, but keeps her eyes on {grupo}.
  - Fallo: The rumour was a little too true. Someone in the room goes pale.
- **Start a debate on whether the Chronicle should exist at all** (intelecto, d 4)
  - Éxito: The whole room argues about freedom of the press. Nobody remembers the matches again.
  - A medias: The debate holds them for a while, until Mara reclaims her question with a smile.
  - Fallo: The argument tangles itself up. Mara sums it up in one line that makes {mejor} look foolish.
- **Decline charmingly, so it sounds like a compliment** (presencia, d 4)
  - Éxito: “It would be unfair to spoil the ending for you, Mara.” She laughs; she likes being refused well.
  - A medias: The refusal is gracious, but it sounds as if there's something to hide.
  - Fallo: The refusal comes out curt. Mara writes something in her notebook and doesn't show it.

**Desenlace · favorable** — A night of wit
- publico: At the Calce Printing House, {grupo} were the best conversation of the night. Mara is already quoting them.
  - si `asiste(teo)`: Teo improvised a verse about ink that Mara begged for the Chronicle. {grupo} left to applause.
- privado: Mara wrote to you the next day: “Your young people think. How rare, how useful.”
  - si `asiste(irene)`: Irene stood watching the presses longer than she needed to. She asked how long a print run takes to dry.

**Desenlace · medias** — A proper enough evening
- publico: {grupo} attended the Calce evening. They were seen paying attention; they weren't heard much.
- privado: Mara isn't quoting them, but she won't forget them either. With her, that's something.

**Desenlace · desaire** — Ink on the tablecloth
- publico: At the Calce Printing House, a remark from {mejor} left the room silent. Mara promises details.
- privado: Best not to give Mara more material this season. She has a long memory, and a press.

## An early-autumn stroll · día 28 · jardines
*paseo · plazas 2–4 · cualquiera*

> Asters in bloom, the river close by, and half of Valcenda pretending to stroll by chance.

Requisito: presencia 3, chisme 2
- \+ Leonora introduces her guests to everyone who passes
- − If it rains, every hem will tell the tale

**Carta**

*Dear Matchmaker,*

My gardens reach their finest week just as the season begins; I suspect the asters want to be seen as well. On Friday I'll open the river walks to anyone who knows how to stroll slowly and watch everyone else without being caught at it. Bring whoever wishes to be seen.

*Leonora Serravalle*

**Desenlace · favorable** — The talk of the walks
- publico: Leonora Serravalle never stopped introducing {grupo}. Half the riverbank knows their names now.
  - si `asiste(isolda)`: Isolda Marén took off her gloves to touch the pond. The Serravalles found it charming; everyone else, scandalous.
- privado: Leonora gave you a little nod as you left: she'll invite them again.
  - si `asiste(nilo) y asiste(isolda)`: Nilo and Isolda talked about caravan insurance for half an hour. She laughed properly, twice.

**Desenlace · medias** — An uneventful stroll
- publico: {grupo} strolled through the Serravalle Gardens. The weather was lovely, and that was the main news.
- privado: No one was bored. No one dazzled. A Friday.
  - si `asiste(celia)`: Celia came back with mud on her hem and didn't speak the whole way home.

**Desenlace · desaire** — Mud and glances
- publico: A stumble by the pond turned {grupo}'s stroll into the joke of the afternoon.
- privado: Leonora was kind about it, which is worse. They'll need to come back on a better footing.

## The opening reception · día 29 · palacio
*recepcion · plazas 2–2 · dama_caballero · OBLIGATORIO*

> All eight will be presented to the Queen. One couple will open the first dance.

Requisito: estatus 4, presencia 4, baile 3
- \+ The whole season watches tonight: whatever happens here will be remembered
- − The master of ceremonies is new, and nervous

**Carta**

*To the Matchmaker of the Season,*

Her Majesty will receive your eight young people on Saturday, at lamp-lighting, in Alto Laurel Palace. She asks you to choose one lady and one gentleman to open the first dance before the court. Name, ease and the absence of trodden toes will all be noted.

*Mireya, Keeper of the Lists*

**Incidente (día 29)** · The master of ceremonies announces {a} and {b} with their houses swapped. Someone laughs at the back; the orchestra waits.

- **Smile and bow as if it were part of the protocol** (presencia, d 4)
  - Éxito: {grupo} bow so naturally that the court wonders who made the mistake. The laughter dies away.
  - A medias: The bow is proper, but the laughter at the back takes its time to fade.
  - Fallo: The smile comes late and forced. The court can tell it stung.
- **Calmly correct the error, before the Queen** (estatus, d 4)
  - Éxito: A correction both kind and firm. The Queen nods: she likes names said properly.
  - A medias: The correction is fair, but it sounds like a rebuke. The master of ceremonies turns red.
  - Fallo: No one hears the correction. Now it looks as if they're arguing in the middle of the hall.
- **Start dancing and let the music cover the error** (baile, d 3)
  - Éxito: A glance at the orchestra and the waltz begins. By the third turn, nobody remembers the announcement.
  - A medias: The waltz starts a little out of step, then finds itself halfway through.
  - Fallo: The orchestra wasn't ready. The first bar sounds like a door slammed badly.

**Desenlace · favorable** — A flawless first dance
- publico: {grupo} opened the Queen's dance with an ease that silenced the tables at the back.
  - si `asiste(saul) y asiste(celia)`: Saúl Veyra and Celia Lobreda opened the dance. A Lobreda and a Veyra together: there's already talk of an engagement that would settle the old mountain-pass quarrel.
  - si `asiste(dario) y asiste(celia)`: Dario Miravés and Celia Lobreda: the most elegant couple the palace has seen in years. Nobody looked at the orchestra.
  - si `asiste(isolda)`: Nobody talked about Isolda Marén's hair. Everyone talked about the way she danced.
  - si `asiste(teo)`: Teo Rovena opened the dance without treading on anyone. Valcenda is celebrating it as a minor miracle.
- privado: The Queen asked after you twice. Mireya made a note of it.

**Desenlace · medias** — A respectable beginning
- publico: {grupo} opened the dance without a stumble and without a sparkle. The Queen applauded just enough.
- privado: Mireya says the palace expected a little more of you. She says it fondly.

**Desenlace · desaire** — A first dance best forgotten
- publico: {grupo}'s opening dance got people talking, and not about its elegance. The court enjoyed itself more than it should have.
- privado: The Queen didn't look at you as she left. You'll have ground to recover.

**Desenlace · sin_asistentes**
- publico: The Matchmaker presented no one to open the dance. The Queen opened it with her chamberlain, and Valcenda took note.

## A dancing lesson · día 30 · academia
*clase · plazas 1–3 · cualquiera*

> Inés teaches this season's waltzes before any orchestra has played them.

- \+ +1 Dance for each attendee (up to 4)
- − Those who already dance beautifully won't learn anything new

**Carta**

*Dear Matchmaker,*

This autumn's waltzes are quicker than last year's, and half of Valcenda will find that out mid-ballroom. On Sunday I'll teach them at the Greda Gate Academy. Send me anyone who'd like to arrive at the ball already knowing where to put their feet. Anyone who already knows, send them anyway: I could use a partner who doesn't tremble.

*Inés*

**Al completarse**
- publico: {grupo} took a dancing lesson with Inés. This autumn's waltzes won't catch them out.
- privado: Inés says they worked hard. Coming from her, that's praise.
  - si `asiste(teo)`: Teo trod on Inés twice and made her laugh three times. She says that's rhythm too.
  - si `asiste(dario)`: Dario, who needs no lessons, stayed to help the others. Inés says he teaches patiently, and that surprised her.
  - si `asiste(celia)`: Celia corrected Inés once, very quietly. Inés agreed with her. Celia went home smiling.
  - si `asiste(irene)`: Irene counted the steps aloud all afternoon. It worked, and nobody dared laugh.
