# Mensaje para ChatGPT: regenerar el elenco con las correcciones (14 oct.)

**Cómo usarlo:**
1. En un chat nuevo, **adjunta la hoja de contacto actual** de los 8 personajes. Sirve como referencia de técnica y proporción.
2. Pega el **bloque 1** (reglas comunes) **una sola vez**.
3. Después pide **un personaje por mensaje**, pegando su bloque del apartado 2.
4. Al final, súbeme las 8 imágenes. Yo mido las cabezas y las igualo con la herramienta si alguna queda distinta.

Las propuestas mías que no salen directamente de tus notas están marcadas con ◆ en la tabla del final. Bórralas si no te convencen.

---

## Bloque 1: reglas comunes (pegar primero)

```text
I attach the current line-up of my 8 game characters. It is my STYLE AND PROPORTION REFERENCE.
I'm going to ask you to redraw each character, one per message, with corrections.

LOCK THESE FOR ALL EIGHT (most important):
1. Same drawing technique as the attached line-up: same ink line, same flat colour with one soft
   shadow per material, same face construction, same level of detail. They must look drawn by the
   same artist on the same day.
2. Same proportions and same distance: every character is shown head to hip, with the same camera
   distance and the same head size. The head (top of hair to chin) is one quarter of the image
   height, eyes at about 20% from the top. Same body-to-head ratio for all (about 7.5 heads tall
   for everyone; height differences are NOT shown in these portraits).
3. Faces are equally attractive and equally detailed; no character is more realistic, more
   stylised or more "anime" than the others.
4. The pose must never hide the face, the outfit or the character's intention. Hands simple and
   readable, placed in the upper half of the body when they act.
5. No visual stereotypes of orientation: all ladies equally refined and feminine, all gentlemen
   equally elegant. Personality shows only in expression, posture and clothes.
6. Men's coats have smooth early-19th-century shoulders: no puffed sleeve heads, no padding.
7. Colour: neutral daylight white balance; whites are pure cool white, never cream; no yellow or
   sepia tint.
8. Output: vertical 2:3 (1024 × 1536), one character, fully transparent background, no text.
Reply "ready" and wait for the first character.
```
